Offline AI Academy

Your AI tutor,
entirely offline.

Study any subject, rehearse an interview, or sit a practice exam. The language model is inside the app, so there is no account to create, no subscription to renew, and nothing to upload. Turn it on in a plane and it works exactly the same.

Three ways to use it

🎓

Study

Tell it the subject and why you are learning it. Attach your notes as a PDF or DOCX and it teaches from them — explanations and examples, not a quiz.

💼

Interview

Give it the role, your experience and your résumé. It runs a realistic mock interview, one question at a time, drawn from what is actually on your CV.

✅

Exam Prep

Multiple choice or written answers. Every answer is marked and the correct one explained before you move on to the next question.

Most study apps are a thin app in front of someone else's server. This one is the model, on your phone, answering you directly.

Why offline matters for studying

The things students put into a tutor are not casual: unfinished coursework, a résumé with an address on it, the topics they know they are weak at, a practice exam they did badly in. None of that needs to be on anyone else's server, and in this app it never is.

  • Your documents are read on the device and never copied anywhere.
  • Conversations are not saved — not to a server, not even to the phone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • It works on a train, on a plane, in an exam hall, in a country with bad data.

Bring your own model, or your own cloud

The bundled model is enough for most studying. If you want something else, you have both options and neither is a paid tier:

OptionWhat it means
Import a GGUFDrop any compatible model file into the app and use it instead. Bigger models, your choice.
Connect a cloud APIYour own key for Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek or Qwen — or a custom endpoint such as Ollama, LM Studio or OpenRouter.

Cloud is off unless you set it up. When it is on, your session text goes to that provider; switch back to the bundled model and the network path closes again.

Made to be used, not configured

  • Every mode is one tap away from anywhere in the app.
  • Light and dark, plus six accent colours.
  • Dictate an answer instead of typing it.
  • Copy or share any explanation before you leave a session.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad on iOS 16.4 or later, and about 800 MB free — most of which is the model itself. Because of that size, iOS installs it over Wi-Fi.

Honest about what it is

A model small enough to run on a phone is excellent at explaining, drilling and rehearsing. It is not a reference work, and it will occasionally be confidently wrong. Check anything that matters against your course material — and attach your own notes, which makes it markedly more accurate.
